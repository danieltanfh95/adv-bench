ADV OU (Generation 3 Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald) Metagame Analysis

FORMAT CONTEXT:
- Generation: 3 (Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald)
- Key Mechanics: Permanent Sand Stream, no physical/special split, 100% accurate Blizzard in hail
- Common Playstyles: Sand teams, stall, balanced, offensive
- Notable Trends: Special walls dominance, setup sweepers, weather wars

TIER STRUCTURE:
- OU: OverUsed (primary competitive tier)
- UUBL: UnderUsed BorderLine
- UU: UnderUsed
- NU: NeverUsed

KEY METAGAME CHARACTERISTICS:
- Tyranitar's Sand Stream is permanent and deals chip damage
- Blissey walls special attackers with massive HP and Special Defense
- Skarmory walls physical attackers with high Defense and reliable recovery
- Salamence and Metagross are dominant sweepers with excellent stats
- Swampert is the premier bulky water with only one weakness
- Jirachi provides utility and flinch hax with Iron Head/Headbutt

COMMON TEAM ARCHETYPES:

1. Sand Teams
- Core: Tyranitar + Pokemon that benefit from Sand
- Strategy: Abuse permanent sand damage and Tyranitar's power
- Common members: Tyranitar, Skarmory, Swampert, Celebi, Magneton

2. Stall Teams
- Core: Blissey + Skarmory + Bulky Pokemon with recovery
- Strategy: Outlast opponents with recovery moves and entry hazards
- Common members: Blissey, Skarmory, Suicune, Forretress, Milotic

3. Offensive Teams
- Core: Setup sweepers + wallbreakers
- Strategy: Overwhelm opponents with immediate offensive pressure
- Common members: Salamence, Metagross, Gengar, Jolteon, Heracross

4. Balanced Teams
- Core: Mixed defensive and offensive elements
- Strategy: Adapt to various matchups with versatile Pokemon
- Common members: Swampert, Celebi, Magneton, Aerodactyl, Zapdos
